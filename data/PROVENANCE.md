# Dataset provenance
Series: Europe Brent Spot Price FOB, USD per barrel. Original publisher: U.S. Energy Information Administration.
Original: https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/rbrteD.htm
CSV distributor: https://github.com/datasets/oil-prices
Downloaded: 2026-10-13 from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datasets/oil-prices/master/data/brent-daily.csv
Included slice: 2015-01-01 through 2025-12-31, limited by available source observations. Actual range: 2015-01-02 to 2025-12-31. Rows: 2793.
Missing prices are not invented or forward-filled. Training removes invalid values and rejects conflicting duplicates.
The distributor describes source U.S. government data as public domain; attribute both EIA and the distributor. Code licensing does not override data rights.
Historical data can be revised. This is not a vintage release archive or live feed. The next trading day means the next available price observation, which can skip holidays.
